RELEASE-BUILDER CHECKLIST
-------------------------

Before distributing a build:

1. Keep LICENSE and this file beside Quiz Processing System.exe.
2. Keep vendor_docs/ and the complete vendor/tesseract and vendor/poppler trees.
3. Do not remove license, notice, copyright, package metadata, or readme files
   from either vendor distribution.
4. Confirm eng.traineddata has the size and SHA-256 recorded above.
5. Review the resolved Python environment for dependencies not listed here.
6. Retain or provide the corresponding source for GPL-covered Poppler binaries
   in the manner required by the distribution's license terms.
